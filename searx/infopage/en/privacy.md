# Privacy Policy

This instance runs [SearXNG]({{ GIT_URL }}), a privacy-respecting metasearch engine.

## What this instance does

- Your search queries are forwarded to the enabled search engines, without your IP address
  or browser fingerprint.
- Cookies are only used to store your preferences (language, theme, enabled engines, ...).
  They are not used for tracking and contain no personal identifiers.
- Result images can be proxied through this instance, so the image hosts never see your IP.

## What this instance does not do

- It does not sell, share or analyse your search history.
- It does not build user profiles and does not use third-party analytics or advertising.

## Logs

Server logs, if enabled by the operator, should not contain search queries or IP addresses.
Aggregated engine statistics (response times, error rates) are collected without any link to a
person.

## Third-party engines

Every enabled engine is a separate service with its own privacy policy. The queries sent to an
engine are subject to that engine's policy. You can disable any engine in the preferences.

## Contact

Questions about this policy should be directed to the operator of this instance.
